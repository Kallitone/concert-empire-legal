1. Play and settings on your device

The game keeps your progress, in-game items, reward and purchase status, and preferences in its Android app storage. Sound, music and vibration preferences are saved on your device. If you enable the offline earnings reminder and allow notifications in Android, the game uses your reminder setting and the time your offline earnings reach their cap to schedule a local notification. You can turn the reminder off in the game or in Android settings.

2. Cloud Save through Google Play Games

When the game is connected to your Google Play Games profile and sync is enabled, it can automatically back up and restore your progress. The game uses a Play Games player ID for this game to find the right save. A backup includes your progress, such as unlocked stages, upgrades, managers, balances, pass status and purchase references, along with save times and a version identifier used to resolve conflicts. Google stores the backup under your Play Games profile.

Use Settings → Cloud Save → Pause cloud sync to stop future syncing on the device. You can play with your local save. Google explains how it handles the profile and game activity associated with it. Play Games privacy settings

3. Purchases through Google Play

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. The game uses the product, purchase status, quantity, purchase time and a purchase reference from Google Play Billing to deliver benefits, recognise eligible purchases and prevent duplicate grants. A derived purchase reference can be included in your game save. Google Play can make order and refund records available to us for purchase support, accounting and dispute handling.

4. Rewarded ads and your privacy choices

When ads are enabled, the game uses Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads. Google’s ads SDK may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, app and ad interactions (such as launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Depending on configuration and device settings, Google may also process other device or account identifiers. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, run advertising analytics and detect fraud.

Where consent is required for advertising, use the Google consent message to choose how your data and device storage may be used. When available, reopen the message from Settings → Ad privacy choices to change or withdraw your choices. Android settings also let you manage the advertising ID. Your choices can affect ad availability and personalisation.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google’s explanation for partner apps

5. Support and legal bases

If you email us, we use your email address, message and the information you choose to send, such as a screenshot or Google Play order number, to help you. Send only the details needed to resolve your request.

The following legal bases apply under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • Providing the game and requested features — performance of our contract or steps at your request, including saving progress, syncing when enabled, providing reminders you activate and delivering purchases (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Advertising — consent for advertising uses that require it, including personalisation and access to device storage or identifiers when required (Article 6(1)(a)). Where permitted, we rely on our legitimate interest in preventing ad fraud and protecting the service for limited processing strictly needed for those purposes (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Support and security — performance of the contract for help with the game or a purchase. For other enquiries, troubleshooting, fraud prevention and dispute handling, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game reliable and responding to players (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations — compliance with applicable accounting, tax and other legal requirements (Article 6(1)(c)).

Cloud Save, purchases and advertising use the data needed for those features. You can play locally with sync paused. To respond to a support request, we may need enough information to identify the issue or the purchase.

6. Who receives data and where it is processed

Google receives data through Play Games, Google Play Billing and AdMob as described above. Where applicable, the advertising partners involved are identified in the consent message. Our email service providers handle support correspondence for us. We may also disclose information when required by law or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Google’s services may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer data outside the European Economic Area, we use applicable transfer mechanisms, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Contact us for details or a copy of applicable safeguards. Google Privacy Policy · Google’s transfer frameworks

The game includes a link to Kallitone. When you open Kallitone or an advertiser’s site, the privacy information of that service applies to your visit.

7. Security, retention and deletion

Local saves are kept in Android app storage. Google Mobile Ads encrypts the information it sends in transit. We limit access to support and purchase records to the people and providers who need it for their work.

  • Local data is kept until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Use Android’s Settings → Apps → Concert Empire: Idle Tycoon → Storage → Clear storage to delete the local save, backup and preferences. The in-game Reset progress feature starts a new game and creates a local backup. Depending on your Android settings, system backups may also retain app data. Manage Android backups
  • Cloud data stays with your Play Games profile while the save is stored there. To remove it, pause syncing on devices that have a copy, then open your Play Games profile settings and choose Delete individual game data for Concert Empire: Idle Tycoon. Use Android storage settings to remove the local copy separately. Contact us if you need help. Google’s deletion instructions
  • Support and purchase records we hold are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request, administer a purchase, handle a related claim or meet an applicable legal retention requirement. Google maintains its own payment, profile and advertising records under its policies. Google’s data retention policy

8. Your rights and contact

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. If we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it for future processing at any time. Email support@kallitone.com to exercise your rights. We may ask for information needed to verify your request.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Romania, this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing. ANSPDCP

A parent or legal guardian can contact us about a child’s data or request deletion. Google Play also offers parental controls and purchase approvals.

9. Changes to this policy

We will publish the latest version on this page. Where required, we will let you know about material changes and ask for new consent before using your data for a purpose that requires it.
