Gameplay

Run the show

Every show starts at the gates. Speed up ticketing, entry, and seating to fill the venue faster, then put the earnings toward your next upgrade.

Open new venues

Grow your income to open clubs, halls, arenas, and stadiums. Each open venue adds to your earnings.

Build your crew

Collect manager cards, level them up, and assign your crew to the gates that need them most.

Invest in research

Use the reviews from your shows to fund research that boosts your venues across the network.
