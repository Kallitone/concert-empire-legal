  • One-time upgrades include No Ads, income multipliers, an offline earnings upgrade and the Review Fund. The highest purchased permanent income multiplier applies.
  • Economy Pass, Business Pass and Backstage each grant 30 days of the listed access or benefits from purchase. The passes are purchased separately and expire automatically. You can buy them again manually when available. Daily rewards are subject to the relevant tasks and claim rules.
  • Crew Pack can be purchased again. Each pack grants 100 Reviews plus six manager cards. Once all managers reach their maximum level, each card converts into 25 Reviews.

Manager cards in a Crew Pack are drawn in three pairs. The game selects the first card of each pair at random and duplicates it for the second card. While you have locked managers, the eligible pool is the locked managers in the role with the fewest unlocked managers among roles that still have locked managers. Ties are resolved in the order Tickets, Entry, Seating, General. Once you have unlocked every manager, the pool is all managers below level 10. Each manager in the current pool has an equal chance of selection for the pair (1 divided by the number of eligible managers). The pool is recalculated before the next pair.

In-game cash, Reviews, Prestige points, cards and other items have value only within the game.
